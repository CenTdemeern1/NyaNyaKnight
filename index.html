  • On your 25th birthday, you are walking to work when you stumble across a strange-looking coin dangling from a string tied to a tree. For some inexplicable reason, you feel an overwhelming urge to stop and paw at it. After a bit of struggling, you finally manage to knock the coin free. Inspecting it, you find that it appears to be nothing special, made from what looks like cheap copper. Yet, just as you are about to toss it aside, a strange feeling washes over you. Something tells you that this coin is important, though you cannot possibly imagine why. Deciding to hold on to it for safekeeping, you slip it into your purrrse and continue on your way.
  • On your 45th birthday, you find yourself feeling a little glum about the years that have passed. As you sit reflecting on your life, you suddenly feel something moving inside your purrrse. Reaching inside, you retrieve the strange coin you found all those years ago, only to discover that it is shivering uncontrollably in your paw! A few moments after you remove it from your purrrse, however, the shivering abruptly stops. Looking closer, you notice something you could swear wasn't there before: a strange engraving of a heart in the center of the coin. You turn it over in your paws, inspecting it from every angle, but find no explanation for this peculiar change. Eventually, you tuck the coin away again, left wondering whether the engraving was always there or not...
  • On your 65th birthday, you find yourself at home, reflecting on the many years that have passed you by. Your thoughts are interrupted when you once again feel the familiar, inexplicable shivering coming from your purrrse. Pulling out the coin, you watch in disbelief as the heart engraved upon its surface begins to stir, slowly coming to life. A soft, otherworldly glow emanates from the heart as strange letters begin to appear upon the coin's surface, etching themselves into the metal one by one until the words "Coin of 9 Lives" are revealed. Although the sudden transformation initially frightens you, the heart's gentle warmth soon eases your fears, leaving you with the curious feeling that the coin has finally revealed a small part of its true nature. You cannot explain what you are witnessing, but one thing becomes increasingly clear: this coin is no ordinary trinket. For a fleeting moment, you feel as though the coin is trying to tell you something, though its meaning remains beyond your understanding. With so few years still ahead of you, you consider pawing the heart to see what will happen...
    By pawing the heart, you will be reborn at age 18, losing all your levels and coins and having to start your journey anew. In exchange, you will gain a special XP multiplier for your catgirl classes and abilities, allowing you to relearn what you once knew at a much faster rate. The multiplier will be equivalent to 2 + (MaxLvl / 10), giving you a greater advantage the further you progressed in your previous life. But what if there is more to the coin than a simple second chance at life? Something tells you that the Coin of 9 Lives may reveal another secret after 2 whole centuries. What wonders might await you should you choose to keep living?

  • Your catgirl instincts were right. The moment you hit the grand age of 200, far surpassing your normal livespan, you hear an wonderous hum coming from the coin. An angelic voice begins to softly speak and proceeds to say: "So you've made it this far, I think you might be her... Are you ready to become cute?"
    If you decide to become cute, ALL of your levels, coins and even max levels will be completely reset. Meaning you will start your next life completely anew with no rebirth multipliers. In exchange for becoming cute, you will unlock a new line of Cutie skills and gain cuteness, which will drastically impact your future catgirl lives and abilities.

  • A thousand years have come and gone and yet, you still remain alive... somehow. You feel your coin shivering again and to your surprise, you see small tendrils full of eyeballs emergin from it. As you are about to drop the coin, you hear calming hum followed by whispers in your head... "You have to embrace the Void..."
    You have unlocked "Angelic Realm" class category along with "Angelic Power" abilities.

  • Ten millenia have passed... most of them spent serving the Divines, yet there is still a glimpse of humanity inside of you. All of the sudden your coin starts to glow in the distance, illuminating darkness around you with the brightness of thousand suns. With the very last remnant of your free will you run to your coin and touch it... "You are now part of something bigger than yourself..."
    You have unlocked "Galactic Council" class category along with "Celestial Powers" abilities.
    By clicking on Transcend, you will be reborn once again, losing all your coins, cutness and current levels. However your max level won't be erased but multiplied with Eternal Recollection ability and you will gain essence, new ability that will boost you even further in your journey.
    Transced will only show up if you have Cosmic Recollection ability unlocked!
